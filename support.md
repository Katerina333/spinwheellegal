## Common questions

### Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline — the wheels, the tools, the Secret Santa draw, the voice control. There is no account and nothing to sign in to. Airplane mode is fine.

### How do I add options to a wheel?

Open a wheel and tap **Edit**, or swipe down on the wheel list to create a new one. You can type options, paste a list, or tap **Dictate options** and say them out loud — "pizza, sushi and tacos" becomes three options.

### How do I spin without touching the screen?

Three ways. Ask **Siri** ("spin my wheel"). Tap the **microphone** on the spin screen and say "spin", "add pizza" or "remove pizza". Or spin from your **Apple Watch**.

### The wheel only has two options and looks like a pie

That is the **Full wheel** setting, and it is on by default: a wheel with a few options repeats them around the rim so it still looks like a prize wheel. The odds are exactly the same — two options are still 50/50 however many times they appear. Turn it off in **Settings → Appearance**.

### Is the spin really random?

Yes. Every spin is a fresh random draw, and no result is weighted or decided in advance. The engine is tested statistically across hundreds of thousands of spins, and every result is recorded in **History** so you can check the spread yourself.

### Can I use my own photos?

Yes. In the editor, add a photo to any option, or use **Add photo options** to turn several pictures into one option each. Photos stay on your device.

### How does Secret Santa work?

Add everyone taking part, optionally block pairs who shouldn't draw each other, then shuffle. Everybody is drawn exactly once and nobody draws themselves. You can tell each person privately by message, or pass the phone around and let each person reveal their own name. If you mark yourself as **This is me**, your Apple Watch will show your person — and only yours.

### Why can I see everyone's assignment?

Because there is no server: the draw happens on the organiser's phone, so the phone knows it. The app says so plainly and keeps the names covered by default. If you'd rather not know, use the pass-the-phone reveal and look away.

### Can I change the colours?

Yes — seven palettes, in light and dark. Pick one when you first open the app, or change it any time in **Settings → Theme**. You can also set a colour on an individual slice in the wheel editor.

### It doesn't speak the winner

Turn on **Settings → Announce winners aloud**. VoiceOver users are always told the winner regardless of that setting.

## Subscriptions

Spin Wheel is free to download and free to use. **Spin Wheel Premium** unlocks unlimited wheels, photo wheels, premium palettes, unlimited voice control and unlimited Secret Santa exchanges.

### Managing or cancelling

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to **Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions** on your iPhone to change or cancel one. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.

**Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.**

### Restoring a purchase

New phone, or reset one? Use **Restore purchases**, on the subscription screen or in **Settings → Premium**.

### Refunds

Refunds are handled entirely by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). We cannot issue them ourselves, but do tell us what went wrong.
